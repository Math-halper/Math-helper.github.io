World History — a pocket timeline

Adapted from the original project's bundled internet/world-history site. This page lives in websites/world-history/site.html.

c. 3000 BCE

Writing appears

Cuneiform in Mesopotamia and hieroglyphs in Egypt let ideas outlive memory. Mathematics follows: counting, calendars, geometry for land and stars.

c. 600 BCE

Greek mathematics

Thales and Pythagoras turn measurement into proof. The theorem a²+b²=c² still bears Pythagoras’ name — try it in the Solver!

820 CE

Algebra gets a name

Al-Khwārizmī writes “al-Kitāb al-Mukhtaṣar fī Ḥisāb al-Jabr” in Baghdad. “Algorithm” comes from his name.

1687

Newton’s Principia

Calculus and universal gravitation arrive in one book, changing physics forever.

1969

Apollo 11

Humans land on the Moon — guided by mathematics computed partly by human “computers” like Katherine Johnson.
