Guide

Your account

Created on first launch, stored only in this browser (salted SHA-256 password hash). Refreshing the page signs you out and asks for your password again, just like signing out. Lock hides the desktop behind your password during a visit.

Windows

Drag title bars to move, drag the bottom-right corner to resize, double-click a title bar to maximise. The panel (bottom) shows pinned + running apps; the active app gets a white underline.

Saving

Notes, files, calendar, contacts, mail, slides, boards, chats and game progress autosave per account. Export a backup in Settings, then Backup before clearing browser data.

Adding & removing things

Use App Center to add your own apps and websites, and Customize to rename, re-icon, reorder, theme or hide almost anything.

Going further

Open the Terminal and type help, or try the Calculator app's solver for things like “solve x+3=7”.
